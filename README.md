# 📱 Add Maths Adventure to Your iPhone/iPad in 2 Minutes

## **For Parents: Setup Instructions**

---

### **What is Maths Adventure?**

Maths Adventure is a 15-day learning app for IGCSE Grade 1 Maths. It:
- Works on iPhone, iPad, and Android
- Tracks your child's progress automatically
- Needs no internet after first download
- Looks and feels like a real app

---

### **How to Install (iPhone/iPad)**

#### **Step 1: Open Safari**
- On your child's iPhone or iPad
- Tap Safari icon (compass)

#### **Step 2: Go to the App Link**
- Your teacher gives you a link (or check email)
- Paste the link in Safari address bar
- Press Go

#### **Step 3: Wait for the Page to Load**
- First load takes 5-10 seconds
- You'll see the purple "Maths Adventure" interface

#### **Step 4: Tap the Share Button**
- At the bottom of Safari screen
- Tap the **Share** icon (box with arrow pointing up)
- Scroll left if you don't see it

#### **Step 5: Tap "Add to Home Screen"**
- Look for this option in the share menu
- Tap it
- A dialog box appears

#### **Step 6: Confirm**
- The app name shows as "Maths Adventure" (or "Maths")
- Tap **"Add"** in the top right corner
- Done! ✅

#### **Step 7: Find the App**
- Go to your home screen
- Swipe left or right to find pages
- You'll see a purple square with a star ⭐
- This is "Maths Adventure"

---

### **How to Use the App**

1. **Tap the purple icon** — app opens fullscreen
2. **Top navigation** — tap to switch between sections:
   - 🗺️ Journey (see all 15 days)
   - 📖 Today (today's lesson)
   - ✏️ Worksheet (practice questions)
   - 🎮 Games (interactive learning)
   - 🏆 Quiz (test your knowledge)
   - 👨‍🏫 Teach Today (parent coaching guide)
   - 💡 Parent Tips

3. **Scroll down** to see more content
4. **Tap buttons** to do activities, check answers, take quizzes
5. **Progress saves automatically** — no need to save manually

---

### **Daily Schedule (30 minutes)**

Each day, spend 30 minutes:

1. **0–5 min**: Warm-up quiz (yesterday's topic)
2. **5–15 min**: New concept + explanation
3. **15–25 min**: Games & practice worksheet
4. **25–30 min**: End quiz + sticker reward

---

### **What to Expect**

✅ The app starts at Day 1  
✅ Complete one day, move to next  
✅ Quiz scores show progress  
✅ Worksheets give instant feedback (green = correct, red = try again)  
✅ Games make learning fun  
✅ On Day 15: Full review + certificate  

---

### **For Android (Phone/Tablet)**

1. Open **Chrome** browser
2. Paste the app link
3. Wait for it to load
4. Tap **⋮** (three dots) in top right
5. Tap **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home Screen"**
6. Tap **"Install"**
7. App appears on home screen

---

### **Important: Help Your Child**

**Do:**
- ✅ Let your child use it independently after setup
- ✅ Check the "Teach Today" section (blue button) — it tells YOU how to explain each topic
- ✅ Practice for 30 minutes daily, at the same time
- ✅ Give stickers/stamps on Day 15 when they complete the course
- ✅ Ask them to explain what they learned that day

**Don't:**
- ❌ Do the worksheet for them
- ❌ Let them skip warm-up quizzes
- ❌ Rush through topics — 30 minutes max per day
- ❌ Skip the games — games help memory

---

### **If the App Doesn't Work**

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| "Add to Home Screen" doesn't appear | Use Safari only (not Chrome on iPhone). Update iOS if very old. |
| App won't open from home screen | Tap it again. It takes 2–3 seconds to launch. |
| Quiz answers feel wrong | Check the "Parent Tips" section for explanations. Age-appropriate difficulty is intentional. |
| Progress wasn't saved | Close and reopen the app. Progress auto-saves but needs app reload to show. |
| Old questions still appear after new day | Clear Safari cache: Settings > Safari > Clear History & Data. Then re-add to home screen. |

### **Your Role: Teach Today Section**

**IMPORTANT:** In the app, there's a blue **"Teach Today"** tab.

This is FOR YOU. Every day it tells you:
- What to teach your child
- Exactly what words to say
- Speed tricks (Vedic & CueMath methods)
- Common mistakes to watch for
- Materials you need (usually household items)

**You don't need to be a maths teacher** — the app tells you exactly what to do.
