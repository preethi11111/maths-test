Tips & Strategies 💡

For parents and children — make learning fun every day

👨‍👩‍👧 Tips for Parents
  • →Keep each session to exactly 30 minutes — attention spans are short at age 5, short focused bursts beat long sessions
  • →Count everything in daily life: stairs, vegetables, passing cars — make numbers feel real and everywhere
  • →Never say "maths is hard" — children mirror your attitude. Say "let's figure this out together"
  • →Use "show me with fingers" before writing any number — motor memory and visual memory lock it in faster
  • →Celebrate effort, not just correct answers — "You tried so hard!" is more powerful than marking wrong
  • →Spend 5 minutes reviewing yesterday before starting — spaced repetition is how the brain stores knowledge long-term
  • →IGCSE tip: expose the child to both word form AND numeral — "seven" and "7" are equally important
  • →Use the quiz at the START of each session (yesterday's topic) and END (today's topic)
⭐ Tips for the Child
  • →Use your fingers — they are your first calculator and always with you!
  • →When you are stuck, say the number out loud — hearing it helps your brain remember
  • →Draw pictures for word problems — turning words into drawings makes it easy
  • →Count backwards too: 10, 9, 8 — it makes subtraction feel like a game
  • →Look for patterns: 2, 4, 6, 8 — every number jumps by 2, like hopping on a path!
  • →When adding, start from the bigger number and count up — it is faster!
